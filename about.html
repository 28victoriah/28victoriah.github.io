Victoria Ho

As a high school junior currently taking Multivariable Calculus, Differential Equations, AP Physics C, and AP Biology, I received the AP Scholar with Distinction Award from the College Board during my sophomore year in 2026. My passion lies in advanced STEM research, and I actively contribute to the Aspiring Scholars Directed Research Program (ASDRP) quantum computing project, the USAYPT physics research team, and the TEAMS engineering competition. For USAYPT, I completed a 2026 research project titled “Eddy Currents and Braking Forces,” helping our school place third overall in February 2026. With my team, I also developed our 2026 TEAMS project, “Engineering the Past,” where we advanced through the state contest to qualify for the National Competition in Washington, D.C. Furthermore, I co-authored the ASDRP research paper, “Optimized pathway for designing small molecules with a hybrid quantum classical generative adversarial network,” and presented these findings at the ASDRP Spring 2026 Research Symposium and Exposition. Following its submission in April 2026, the paper was approved for formal peer review at the National High School Journal of Science (NHSJS) and is currently undergoing revisions.

As an active Math Club member, my competition background includes an Honorable Mention at the Berkeley Math Tournament (BMT), a Distinguished Honorable Mention team award at the Stanford Math Tournament (SMT), and participation in both the Harvard-MIT Math Tournament (HMMT) and the Caltech Math Meet (CMM). I also placed in the Silver Division of the All Girls Math Online Tournament in 2023. These experiences have deeply advanced my problem-solving skills outside the standard curriculum. Beyond STEM, I am fascinated by Latin, a classical language that serves as my key to exploring ancient mythology and culture while tracing the evolution of modern languages. An active participant of JCL, I placed 10th nationally in Reading Comprehension: Prose (Level 3) in the NJCL Convention and 3rd in the Ludi Latin Grammar Test (Grade 9). On the National Latin Exam, I consistently received Gold Medals and Summa Cum Laude honors in 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2026, alongside a Silver Medal in 2025. Additionally, on the Pegasus National Mythology Exam, I earned Summa Cum Laude honors in 2024, Maxima Cum Laude in 2022, and a Silver Medal in 2023, followed by a Silver Medal during the 2025 National Etymology Exam cycle.

Driven by community service and leadership, I am a member of the National Honor Society, lead a summer math workshop at my local public library, and mentor younger children through both the library and my school's Eagle Buddies program. For these efforts, I received the Bronze-level Community Engagement Award for 2025–2026, as well as Honor in Community Service Certificates for the 2022–2023 and 2023–2024 academic years.

In addition, I am a member of Silicon Valley Youth Climate Action (SVYCA) and participated in the 2026 SVYCA Summit. Through this platform, I aim to share my research findings on plastic degradation, stay informed on critical climate issues, and collaborate with other youth leaders to raise public awareness and champion actionable environmental change.

Finally, as a self-taught artist, I love relaxing through digital drawing, watercolor, and sketching. My creative efforts were recognized with a Silver Key in Digital Art at the 2025 Scholastic Art & Writing Awards.
