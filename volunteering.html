Founder & Lead Volunteer, Summer Math Workshop Program (March 2026–Present)

I independently initiated, designed, and led the Summer Math Workshop Program at the local public library. In March 2026, I wrote a formal proposal and submitted an approval request to the library's volunteer department; the program was successfully approved in May 2026. This new, independent initiative is completely distinct from the regular library programming I joined in 2024.

The program is designed to develop students' mathematical understanding through monthly, one-hour workshops held during the summer. To foster deep discussion and collaborative learning, enrollment is capped at six students per session. Each workshop follows a structured format: concept review, independent work time, group discussion, and solution sharing. Students are consistently encouraged to share their unique strategies and thinking with one another.

The curriculum consists of six specialized topics:

1. Multiples and Divisibility

2. Sequences

3. Series

4. Counting

5. Probability

6. Geometry

To date, I have successfully completed two of the three workshops (held in June, July, and August), with the next session scheduled for August. I have attached my proposal slide deck and the librarian's feedback for your review and reference.

Member, Teens Reach Youth Council – Bascom Branch, San José Public Library (June 2024–Present)

Teens Reach is the official youth council of the San José Public Library system, providing a platform for leadership, community service, and youth advocacy. Having deeply benefited from library services as a child, I joined the Bascom Branch chapter to give back to my community and directly shape teen-centric spaces. Through this program, I earn community service hours while developing professional skills, acting as a library advocate, and spearheading peer-led initiatives. My involvement also allows me to bridge local community service with broader environmental advocacy, including participating in regional climate research initiatives. Since joining in June 2024, my contributions have included:

Academic Mentorship: Created and led interactive Summer Math Workshops to keep younger students engaged and academically confident during school breaks.

Youth Program Supervision: Managed and facilitated popular children’s enrichment programs, including LEGO Fun and Origami workshops, which cultivate critical thinking, creativity, and perseverance in a fun learning environment.

Literacy Advocacy: Authored detailed children’s book reviews to guide young readers and help foster a lifelong love for reading within the community.

Operational Support: Assisted the Friends of the Library book sales by sorting and categorizing inventory to raise funding for branch resources.

Outreach: Promoted the library’s Summer Learning program by designing seasonal decorations and marketing posters to increase community participation

Community Welfare: Attended regular council meetings, advocated for youth resources, and contributed to local food donation drives.

Cross-Organizational Leadership: Represented the library youth perspective by participating in the annual Silicon Valley Youth Climate Action (SVYCA) Summit on August 8, 2026.
